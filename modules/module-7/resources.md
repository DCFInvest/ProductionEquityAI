# Resources

## Templates and checklists
- **Rollout plan template:** Goals, guardrails, stages, approvals, rollback, communication plan.
- **Monitoring matrix:** SLIs/SLOs by segment (e.g., region, demographic, device), thresholds, owners.
- **Incident playbook:** Detection, triage, user communication, remediation, and verification steps.
- **Retrospective guide:** Prompts for equity outcomes, stakeholder feedback, and system/process changes.

## Suggested readings
- NIST AI RMF sections on measurement and monitoring.
- Case studies on staged rollouts and incident response in ML systems.
- Research on distribution shift detection and fairness-aware monitoring.
- Public postmortems from organizations addressing algorithmic bias incidents.

## Tools to explore
- Feature flagging platforms with segment-based rollouts.
- Drift detection libraries and fairness dashboards.
- Synthetic data generation for stress-testing underrepresented segments.
