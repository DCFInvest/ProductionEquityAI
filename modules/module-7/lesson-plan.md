# Lesson Plan

## Session 1: Rollout strategy and equity guardrails (90 minutes)
- **Mini-lecture (20 min):** Staged rollouts, canary releases, and feature flags for ML services.
- **Case study (15 min):** Postmortem of an inequitable model launch and how guardrails could have prevented harm.
- **Workshop (40 min):** Draft rollout goals, equity guardrails, and stop conditions in small groups.
- **Share-out (15 min):** Groups present guardrails and receive feedback.

## Session 2: Observability and incident readiness (90 minutes)
- **Demo (25 min):** Monitoring signals for performance, drift, and fairness; alert routing and dashboards.
- **Exercise (30 min):** Define SLOs/SLIs and alert thresholds for protected segments.
- **Drill (25 min):** Incident response tabletop using a simulated biased prediction spike.
- **Reflection (10 min):** Document gaps in monitoring or playbooks.

## Session 3: Measuring impact and retrospectives (60 minutes)
- **Lecture (15 min):** Measuring user impact, legal/compliance reporting, and continuous improvement loops.
- **Workshop (35 min):** Build a retrospective template with equity-focused prompts.
- **Wrap-up (10 min):** Commit to follow-up actions and owners.

## Instructor prep checklist
- Pre-populate monitoring dashboards with synthetic signals for exercises.
- Prepare incident simulation data and logs for the tabletop drill.
- Print or share templates for rollout planning and retrospectives.
