# Module 7: Responsible Production Rollout

This module guides learners through planning and executing a responsible rollout for machine learning systems focused on production equity. The content emphasizes reliability, fairness, monitoring, and stakeholder communication during deployment.

## Learning objectives
- Translate business objectives and equity requirements into production readiness criteria.
- Design rollout plans that balance experimentation speed with risk mitigation.
- Implement observability practices that surface bias, drift, and reliability signals.
- Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders and incident response procedures.
- Evaluate rollout outcomes and incorporate lessons learned into continuous improvement.

## Prerequisites
- Completion of modules covering data foundations, modeling, evaluation, and governance.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, model monitoring concepts, and basic incident management.

## Deliverables
- A written rollout plan that includes risk analysis, stakeholder roles, and monitoring strategy.
- A playbook for incident response and escalation that centers user impact.
- A retrospective template to capture lessons learned post-rollout.

## Assessment criteria
- **Clarity**: Rollout goals, guardrails, and success metrics are explicitly defined.
- **Equity focus**: Risk analysis and monitoring cover disparate impact and representativeness.
- **Operational rigor**: Deployment stages, approvals, and rollback procedures are actionable.
- **Communication**: Stakeholder map, cadences, and post-incident reporting are documented.
- **Reflection**: Retrospective prompts tie outcomes back to equity objectives and future improvements.
