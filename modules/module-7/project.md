# Capstone Assignment: Responsible Rollout Plan

Create a rollout plan for an ML-powered feature that must meet explicit equity requirements.

## Scenario
Your team is launching an eligibility model that prioritizes outreach for community services. Historical data reflects enrollment biases across regions and demographics. Leadership has committed to minimizing disparate impact and ensuring transparent reporting during the rollout.

## Requirements
1. **Context & goals**
   - Describe the feature, intended users, and expected outcomes.
   - Define success metrics with thresholds for both overall performance and segment-level fairness.
2. **Risk analysis**
   - Identify potential harms (representation gaps, disparate error rates, feedback loops, legal/compliance risks).
   - Rate severity/likelihood and propose mitigations.
3. **Rollout plan**
   - Deployment stages (shadow, canary, phased expansion) with entry/exit criteria.
   - Approvals and sign-offs (product, legal, data science, affected stakeholders).
   - Rollback triggers and procedures.
4. **Monitoring & alerting**
   - SLIs/SLOs that include segment-level performance and fairness indicators.
   - Alert routing, on-call rotations, and escalation timelines.
   - Dashboard sketches or tables that define signals and dimensions.
5. **Incident response**
   - Playbook for investigation, user communication, and remediation when equity issues arise.
   - Roles and responsibilities, including decision-makers and user advocates.
6. **Retrospective plan**
   - Template for post-rollout review with prompts on equity outcomes, user impact, and process improvements.

## Submission format
- 3–5 page written plan (or slide deck) with diagrams/tables as needed.
- Link to supporting materials (dashboards, queries, checklists) if available.

## Evaluation rubric (20 points)
- **Context & goals (4 pts):** Clear objectives, metrics, and guardrails.
- **Risk analysis (4 pts):** Comprehensive harms, mitigations, and prioritization.
- **Rollout plan (4 pts):** Concrete stages, approvals, and rollback procedures.
- **Monitoring & alerting (4 pts):** Actionable SLIs/SLOs with equity-aware signals.
- **Incident response & retrospective (4 pts):** Credible playbooks and learning loops.
