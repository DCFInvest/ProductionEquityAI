# Module 2: Data Equity Foundations

## Overview
Module 2 focuses on the data practices required to build equitable AI systems for content production. It guides participants through sourcing, auditing, and governing datasets so that downstream generative models reduce, rather than reinforce, harm.

## Learning objectives
- Distinguish between representational, allocative, and procedural harms that can arise from training data.
- Conduct a data equity audit to identify gaps in demographic coverage and contextual nuance.
- Design data collection guidelines that respect consent, privacy, and community norms.
- Establish governance workflows for dataset documentation, access controls, and ongoing monitoring.

## Agenda
1. **Why data equity matters** – case studies of inequitable content generation and systemic root causes.
2. **Mapping stakeholders** – collaborators, impacted communities, data stewards, and reviewers.
3. **Data audit walkthrough** – schema review, sampling strategies, bias detection checks.
4. **Governance playbook** – versioning, approvals, redaction, and incident response triggers.
5. **Hands-on lab** – complete a mini audit using a provided data slice and propose mitigations.

## Key activities
- **Pre-work:** Read the provided data equity primer and annotate examples of potential harm.
- **Workshop:** Facilitate a stakeholder mapping session using the supplied template.
- **Lab:** Run the audit checklist on a sample dataset, document findings, and prioritize follow-ups.
- **Retro:** Debrief on what worked, gaps discovered, and policy updates required.

## Deliverables
- Completed data equity audit report.
- Updated dataset documentation (Data Cards/Model Cards section for data) with identified risks.
- Governance checklist tailored to the team’s production workflows.

## Resources
- Data audit checklist template
- Stakeholder mapping worksheet
- Links to relevant privacy, consent, and licensing guidelines

## Assessment
- Participation in workshop and lab activities.
- Quality and completeness of the audit report.
- Practical mitigation plan aligned to governance standards.
